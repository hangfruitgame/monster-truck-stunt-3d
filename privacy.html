Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Welcome to Monster Truck Games - Stunt 3D ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about any information collected through the game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifying information directly, such as your real name, physical address, or phone number. We do not require account registration or login to play the Game.

However, to serve advertisements, optimize game stability, and reward players, our third-party advertising partners may automatically collect certain device and usage data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising Identifier (such as IDFA, subject to your App Tracking Transparency permission) or non-personal device IDs.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, interactions with video rewards, and gameplay session events.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Crash logs, framerate reports, and general performance diagnostics.
Google AdMob Integration

We utilize Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and cookies/mobile identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

Our game integrates trusted third-party SDKs that have their own privacy practices:

  • Google AdMob: For monetization and rewarded ads. For more information on how Google processes user data, please consult Google's Advertising Policies and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / iOS Services: Standard operating system frameworks for in-app purchases and native share features.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising. You have the full right to decline this request. If declined, advertisements will still be shown, but they will be contextual rather than personalized.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at ahmedmouzzamgame@gmail.com, and we will promptly remove such information.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us device information for advertising and crash diagnostics. All network communications with our third-party ad partners occur over encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
