Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to Monster Truck Games - Stunt 3D ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency about our data practices.

1. Data Not Collected

We respect your privacy. Monster Truck Games - Stunt 3D does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location.
  • No User Accounts: No registration, login, or user profiles are required to play the Game.
  • No Tracking Identifiers: We do not collect, request, or access your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or any identifier used for tracking.
  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, unlocked vehicles, and high scores are saved locally on your device only and are never transmitted to external servers.
Google AdMob Integration (Non-Personalized Ads)

The Game utilizes Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and opt-in rewarded video advertisements. Advertisements served are non-personalized and contextual. AdMob does not track you across apps and websites from other companies in this Game.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

Our game integrates trusted third-party SDKs strictly for gameplay monetization and basic platform functionality:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying contextual advertisements. For details on Google's privacy practices, please review Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / iOS Services: Standard iOS operating system frameworks for game execution and native sharing features.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, Monster Truck Games - Stunt 3D does not track users across other companies' apps and websites. Because our game does not perform cross-app tracking or collect identifiers for tracking purposes, the AppTrackingTransparency (ATT) permission prompt is not triggered or required.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you have any questions regarding our practices, please contact us at ahmedmouzzamgame@gmail.com.

5. Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data or user accounts on remote servers, your personal data is not exposed to external security risks. Any ad delivery communications occur over standard encrypted HTTPS connections.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
