Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I unlock new monster trucks?
You can unlock high-performance monster trucks by earning in-game coins through completing challenging stunt levels, or by choosing to watch quick optional video rewards in the truck selection garage.
2. Can I play the game offline?
Yes! All stunt mega ramps and racing levels are fully playable offline without an active internet or Wi-Fi connection. (An internet connection is only needed if you wish to watch optional video ads for extra bonus coins).
3. How do the controls work on iPhone and iPad?
Use the right-side accelerator and brake pedals to control your speed. When your truck is flying through the air off mega ramps, use the dual tilt sliders or buttons to adjust pitch and balance your truck for a smooth landing!
4. Why is there no sound in the game?
Please make sure your device's physical mute/silent switch is turned off, and ensure your device volume is turned up. Also check the in-game settings to confirm sound effects and music are turned on.
5. How do I report a bug or suggest a new feature?
Simply send an email to ahmedmouzzamgame@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue or your suggestion.
